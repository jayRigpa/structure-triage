The Eleven Structures:

This is a companion to your reading — the same eleven structures the facilitator works from, translated out of the framework's technical vocabulary into ordinary practitioner language. Nothing has been added; specialized terms have been rendered into their closest common meaning, and references to the framework's internal research stages have been replaced by the claims they pointed to.

Two things to hold while reading. First: structures are not types of people. A structure is the pattern currently carrying the weight in your practice life — what the framework calls load-bearing, like the wall that holds the building up. Structures change with development, and most practitioners move through several. Second: where a practice is said to "lean toward" C, N, T, or W, that refers to the four endpoint families below — because different lineages do not obviously arrive at the same summit, and choosing a practice is quietly choosing a direction. The framework thinks that choice should be made with eyes open.

The four endpoints, briefly. C — cessation: the path of deep stillness and release, where experience itself falls away (the classical Theravada trajectory). N — nondual recognition: life continues, recognized as the display of aware nature (Dzogchen, Zen, Advaita). T — devotional union: union with the Beloved (Teresa, John of the Cross, the Sufis) — and the framework honestly does not know whether this is a distinct summit or nondual recognition lived in a personal key. W — naturalness: effortless action and frictionless participation in life (the Taoist sages).

A note on psychedelics, breathwork, and dark retreat, which appear in several structures: the framework treats them as amplifiers, not paths. They can powerfully loosen the mind's construction, but they don't by themselves supply the recognition, the integration, or the ethics. They help when three conditions hold: enough inner steadiness to meet what arises, support proportional to intensity, and a regular practice that revisits in ordinary life what was glimpsed in the amplified state. Without those, they produce impressive experiences without understanding, or destabilization without repair. For anyone carrying significant trauma, they come late in the sequence — never first.

Structure 1 — Identification Concentrated in Thought and Narrative

Presentation. You are your story and your positions. Thoughts don't feel like events passing through — they feel intensely personal: my thoughts, me thinking. When someone challenges your views it lands as a threat to your existence, not a disagreement. Rumination and self-referential mental commentary dominate the inner landscape. What's holding the weight: the deep habit of experiencing mental investment as identity — what I care about and believe feels like a fact about who I am — running on the mind's constant loop of rehearsing and replaying.

Primary practices. Vipassana and noting practice aimed at thought itself — watching thoughts arrive, as happenings, which directly contradicts the felt sense of authoring and owning them. Daily-life mindfulness with a bias toward catching the narrator in the act. Somatic anchoring as a counterweight — returning attention below the neck, again and again, which starves the thinking loop of fuel. Self-inquiry is available but carries a real risk here: for a thought-identified system, inquiry easily becomes more thinking — the question gets answered instead of looked from. Gentle unfolding work in the style of Aletheia helps when it tracks the felt sense underneath the story rather than the story itself. Endpoint note: this work is neutral — it doesn't yet commit you toward stillness (C) or recognition (N).

Why it works. The sense that thoughts are "me" is an investment the mind makes — a grip. Repeatedly observing thoughts as arrivals shifts attention from what the thought says to the fact that it appeared, and the "mine" label starts to become visible as a label. How it goes wrong: analysis masquerading as observation — the telltale sign is that practice sessions produce conclusions instead of seeings — and collecting insights as fresh material for the story of me. Signs it's working: thoughts arrive with less grip; gaps between thoughts get noticed without being hunted; you can hold a position without defending it. When to change course: if noting turns into commentary or rumination intensifies, drop to body-first practice for a while. Sequencing: a modest base of calm-and-stability (enough to observe without getting swept up) should come first; deep absorption isn't required.

Structure 2 — Identification Concentrated in Body and Affect

Presentation. Emotions aren't had — they're been. Bodily states dictate what's real: "I am anxious" as identity, not weather. Emotional storms arrive with total conviction. What's holding the weight: deep investment in body-level feelings as self, plus the conviction that what you feel is caused entirely by the thing you're feeling it about.

Primary practices. Somatic inquiry with titration — contacting sensation in small, tolerable doses, investigating its texture rather than its story. Vipassana on the feeling-tone layer — the subtle pleasant/unpleasant register where grasping and pushing-away first take hold. IFS parts work, because emotion-dominant systems are usually parts-forward, and unblending — stepping back so you can relate to a feeling rather than be taken over by it — is the direct move out of identification. Compassion practice turned inward, softening your relationship to the feeling instead of fighting it. Breathwork with care: it amplifies emotion, which serves contact when you have the capacity to meet it, and floods you when you don't.

Why it works. Body-level feelings carry the densest sense of "me" in the whole system; meeting them at a tolerable dose lets the grip renegotiate itself without triggering the system's protection. How it goes wrong: dissociation mistaken for equanimity — the felt marker is numbness and distance rather than contact and ease, and learning to tell those apart is diagnostic gold — and catharsis-seeking, where emotional discharge substitutes for actually seeing. Signs it's working: feeling arises at full intensity and is visible as arising; the gap between sensation and story widens; storms get shorter. When to change course: if practice consistently floods or numbs you, move to Structure 10's approach (parts first). Sequencing: this work comes before intensive retreats — a body-identified system sent into strong-determination sitting without titration skills is a known casualty pattern.

Structure 3 — Control and Vigilance

Presentation. Safety through monitoring. Attention runs tight by default; an inner manager supervises everything — including relaxation itself. Meditation becomes one more performance to get right. What's holding the weight: the felt sense of being the doer, fused with a background conviction that danger is always near — doing as defense.

Primary practices. The path of letting go — but indirectly. Direct surrender instruction fails here, predictably, because the surrender gets performed by the controller: "doing non-doing," the doer surviving as the one who surrenders. The sequence that works: first, IFS with the vigilant protector — vigilance is protective work, and it must be negotiated with, not overridden; a protector relaxes when its job is honored and its fear is met, never when commanded. Then shikantaza or open awareness — practices with nothing to monitor and no score to keep. Breathwork has a paradoxical use: it gives the controller a task (the protocol) whose execution produces states the controller cannot manufacture, demonstrating from the inside that control is optional. Tight-focus concentration practice is contraindicated as a primary practice — it arms the very pattern you're working with. Ethical work helps too, by lowering the background sense of threat that funds the vigilance. Endpoint note: letting-go work leans toward recognition (N) and naturalness (W).

Why it works. The sense of being the doer drops when appropriate action is discovered to continue without the doer's supervision — things keep getting done, and the supervisor is caught being decorative. How it goes wrong: white-knuckled letting-go; surrender as strategy (with one eye on results — which is control in surrender's clothes); collapse mistaken for release. Signs it's working: effort discovered to be optional mid-action; the exhale you didn't decide on; supervision arriving late to actions already underway — and noticing its own lateness. When to change course: if every relaxation practice turns into a new performance, stop practicing relaxation entirely and work the protector relationship until relaxation is offered unprompted.

Structure 4 — Craving and Seeking

Presentation. The chase — often aimed at awakening itself. State-hunting, retreat-hopping, teacher-shopping, glimpse-collecting; each next attainment carries the charge the last one lost. What's holding the weight: the mind's standing strategy of grasping, pointed at the path — the self keeping itself in business through the project of its own transcendence.

Primary practices. Inquiry into the seeker — who wants this? what does the wanting feel like right now, as raw sensation? Shikantaza sat honestly, under its own logic of nothing-to-attain. Contentment and gratitude practices, which starve the signal of lack the chase runs on. Deliberately unremarkable daily-life practice — doing the dishes without making them a path event. Jhana is double-edged here: skillfully framed, absorption gives the system a satisfaction deep enough that craving can finally be examined from the far side of it (the classical Theravada logic); unskillfully framed, it's the finest state-candy available and feeds the chase. The framing decides which. Endpoint note: this structure faces a live fork — handling it through dispassion leans toward cessation (C); handling it through inquiry into the seeker leans toward recognition (N). Both work. Choose knowingly.

Why it works. Craving is a strategy of forcing a desired experience onto the present; both investigating its felt texture and repeatedly tasting enough-ness expose the strategy as a strategy. How it goes wrong: renunciation as inverted seeking — chasing the state of not-chasing — and apathy misread as contentment. Signs it's working: the seeking energy relaxes without any collapse of aliveness or curiosity — you still practice, but practice stops being a hostage negotiation. When to change course: if inquiry into the seeker becomes one more acquisition project, switch to service, ethics, and compassion — orientations that spend the energy outward.

Structure 5 — Aversion and Collapse

Presentation. Avoidance as architecture — of feeling, conflict, memory, intensity. Transcendence used as an exit (spiritual bypass). Flatness, or a chronic subtle bracing. "Spiritual" equanimity that is actually distance. What's holding the weight: the mind's standing strategy of pushing away, running invisibly — experience pre-filtered to exclude what it doesn't want, while the exclusion is blamed on the things excluded.

Primary practices. The path of the heart as softener first: metta and compassion practice, which change your stance toward the avoided before you approach it. Titrated somatic contact with the avoided — graduated exposure, in doses. Deliberately entering situations that trigger the pattern, with the capacity to step back from what arises already in place — one long-term practitioner reports this as a deliberate multi-year practice: seek the trigger, then investigate what comes up. IFS for the wounded, exiled material behind the avoidance. Vipassana works when aimed at the aversion itself as the object — investigating the flinch, rather than the thing flinched from.

Why it works. Aversion maintains itself by keeping its object out of clear view; anything that brings the object into tolerable contact while the pushing-away is watched exposes the strategy. The suffering you add on top of pain becomes visible as something fired, not something suffered. How it goes wrong: metta as bypass — loving-kindness sprayed over the avoided from a safe distance; forced acceptance, which is aversion wearing acceptance's clothes (the marker is a subtle deadline-energy: "I'm accepting this so it will leave"); flooding, when the dosing is skipped. Signs it's working: the avoided can be visited voluntarily; flatness gives way to grief or fear that moves — and further along, difficult feeling at full intensity carrying a kind of embedded poignancy rather than fight. When to change course: persistent flooding or shutdown means Structure 10's sequencing applies. Sequencing: where bypass is prominent, honesty and ethical work often must come first — avoidance usually has social accomplices.

Structure 6 — Strong Concentration, Weak Insight

Presentation. Reliable access to absorption, deep states, refined stillness — and the sense of self returns from every one of them intact. Years of attainment; character unchanged. States as destinations. What's holding the weight: deep states are being ridden for their scenery — the quieting of mental construction is achieved, but its absence is never recognized as the absence of a construction.

Primary practices. Turn the built instrument onto the machinery itself. Vipassana launched from the stability that follows absorption (the classical jhana-then-insight sequence exists precisely for this structure). Inquiry posed at the edge of absorption, where the sense of self is thin enough to watch reassemble — "what returns first?" is a complete practice here. Pointing-out instruction, for which this structure is the ideal recipient: the stability to actually look where the finger points. Noting the transitions into and out of states, rather than the states themselves. Endpoint note: this structure sits directly at the fork — the same stability serves the path of cessation (C) or recognition within experience (N), and these may genuinely be different summits. Choose with eyes open rather than by your lineage's default.

Why it works. A contrast is informative only when it's read; these practices convert state-changes from experiences to be enjoyed into data about how experience is built. How it goes wrong: state-collecting resumes at a finer grain; or the observer-position hardens — the watcher of the assembly is still a frame, and it can become the new hiding place. Signs it's working: states become boring in the best way — informative rather than magnetic; what you sought as refuge, you now investigate as exhibit. When to change course: if a decade of deep states has produced no change in daily character, the structure may actually be seeking (Structure 4) wearing concentration's clothes — rediagnose.

Structure 7 — Strong Insight, Weak Stabilization

Presentation. Genuine glimpses, real insight, the capacity to recognize — and ordinary life proceeds largely unrenovated. Oscillation between the seen mode and full re-entanglement. The retreat-self and the Tuesday-self are barely acquainted. What's holding the weight: the moments of clear seeing have genuinely occurred — but the thousands of situation-specific habits and assumptions, each wired to its own context, haven't yet been visited by the seeing.

Primary practices. The center of gravity moves from cushion to world. Daily-life mindfulness as the primary practice rather than a supplement. Deliberate exposure as a named practice: intentionally entering the contexts that re-trigger the old pattern, then meeting what arises with the capacities built elsewhere — this is one long-term practitioner's reported core strategy, and the framework endorses it as the structurally correct response to the problem: the pattern lives in specific contexts, and nothing visits contexts except visiting them. Places of ethical friction worked as curriculum — everywhere you insist, something hasn't been examined. IFS for the specific parts that reliably take over. Relationship and work treated as the retreat. Formal sits maintain the instrument; they are no longer where the work is.

Why it works. Integration is re-weighting the system's habits one context at a time; there is no shortcut that skips the contexts. How it goes wrong: exposure without enough capacity to step back — a trigger met while fully identified is a rehearsal of the pattern, not a revealing of it; the dose makes the medicine. And mistaking tolerance for integration — gritting through the trigger rather than seeing how it assembles. Signs it's working: recovery time shrinks; the noticing moves earlier — from after the episode, to during it, to at the moment it arises; triggers begin to produce curiosity before they produce contraction. When to change course: if the same context defeats you indefinitely, the habit in question is probably protective — route through Structure 10's parts-negotiation rather than more exposure.

Structure 8 — Devotional Temperament

Presentation. The heart is the organ of practice. Love, longing, and relationship carry more voltage than investigation; impersonal framings feel arid. You practice best toward. What's holding the weight: nothing broken — the path of the heart is simply native here; this system's investment transfers readily and naturally to a beloved object.

Primary practices. Devotion, prayer, surrender-toward, guru-yoga-style practices, compassion as the path itself rather than an adjunct, sacred music and beauty as legitimate technology. The mechanism deserves plain statement: devotion pours the self's investment into the Beloved until the investor is spent — love as the solvent of the lover. Endpoint note, stated with full honesty: this route leans toward devotional union (T), and the framework genuinely does not know whether that union is a distinct summit or nondual recognition (N) lived in a personal key. The traditions themselves fork here: some press through union toward the annihilation of even the lover; others rest in union, as marriage. A devotional practitioner should know the fork exists and that this framework cannot settle it. For those who find that even union has become furniture, the negative way — Eckhart, the Cloud of Unknowing — offers a within-tradition path onward.

Why it works. For this temperament, love naturally outweighs self-concern; fighting that with dry inquiry wastes the system's strongest current. How it goes wrong: idealization and transference, especially toward living teachers — the well-documented harm pattern; devotion curdling into specialness ("the Beloved's beloved"); the relationship itself defended as furniture. Signs it's working: the love generalizes — from its object outward, until the Beloved's face is increasingly everywhere, and the practice continues without its original scaffolding. When to change course: if devotion has stabilized into comfortable relationship without further movement, and further movement is wanted, introduce inquiry into the lover — who loves? — gently, as the tradition's own deepening rather than a betrayal of it.

Structure 9 — Skeptical and Inquiry Temperament

Presentation. Nothing is accepted unexamined. Doubt is the native faculty; devotion feels dishonest, and instruction lands as a set of claims to be tested. Often intellectually trained. What's holding the weight: nothing broken here either — the path of looking-until-the-search-fails is native; this system will look, hard, if given something worth looking at.

Primary practices. Self-inquiry and nondual inquiry as primary. Koan work — especially the who-am-I family, where doubt is turned into an instrument and given a target it cannot resolve by thinking. Aletheia-style unfolding, which suits this temperament by treating each layer's report as data rather than doctrine. Pointing-out instruction with the explicit invitation to test rather than believe. The one instruction that matters most: the search for the self must fail vividly rather than conclude — "there's no self, QED" is a thought, and arriving at it is this temperament's signature failure. Endpoint note: leans strongly toward recognition (N).

Why it works. This path runs on honest looking, and skepticism is honest looking with good brakes. Searching for the searcher and vividly not finding one is this temperament's natural doorway precisely because it is an observation, not a claim to accept. How it goes wrong: the conceptual conclusion; doubt as identity — the skeptic surviving as the one who has seen through everything, including, note, as the holder of sophisticated frameworks about awakening (this document is a candidate host); endless refinement of the map. Signs it's working: questions stop resolving into answers and start resolving into seeing; the moment of looking-and-not-finding acquires texture and weight; the framework gets lighter, not heavier. When to change course: if inquiry has become entirely comfortable, it has become thinking — introduce the heart's register (compassion, gratitude, beauty), not as belief but as data-gathering in a channel this system undervalues.

Structure 10 — Trauma-Loaded and Parts-Dominated Systems

Presentation. Many strong inner parts with little trust among them. Flooding or shutdown under meditative pressure. A history that includes developmental or acute trauma. Strong protectors. Practice attempts that reliably end in destabilization. What's holding the weight: a structural fact — protective patterns cannot let go under direct dissolution pressure; the system correctly reads that pressure as threat, and tightens or fragments.

This structure overrides all others in sequencing. Wherever it is present alongside any structure above, it goes first.

Primary practices. IFS or equivalent parts work as the primary path, full stop — unblending, building relationship with protectors, permission-based access to the wounded parts they guard. Somatic titration and resourcing. Co-regulation: skilled, safe relationship as practice — this system heals in contact, not in isolation. Ethics and boundaries as safety infrastructure. Contraindicated until substantial renegotiation has happened: intensive noting retreats, strong-determination formats, dark retreat, high-dose psychedelic work, and any practice framed as dissolving the self — because the self in question is load-bearing scaffolding over unmet material. Those practices become available later, and often work beautifully then. (The risk profile here is clinically well-established; the mechanistic explanation is more theoretical.)

Why it works. Protectors relax when their function is honored and their fear is met — voluntarily, never under command. And unblending is de-identification: this path is not a detour from awakening work but a form of it, conducted at the pace the system consents to. How it goes wrong: the parts work gets colonized by a spiritualized inner manager ("healing" as the new performance); premature graduation. Signs it's working: protectors soften unprompted; internal trust accumulates; capacity for the other structures' practices comes online organically. When to change course: forward, into any structure above, when disruptions no longer cost you days.

Structure 11 — Late Stage: Transparent Priors (Invisible Assumptions)

Presentation. The construction of experience is largely seen as it arises; life proceeding on its own, without a doer, is the stable daily mode; identification still arises in specific situations, is noticed, and is met. And the practitioner correctly infers that assumptions remain which filter experience without ever showing up as objects in it — like a lens you look through and therefore can never look at. This is the reported current edge of a long-term practitioner who helped calibrate this framework, and the framework treats it as the characteristic problem of the stabilization phase: the invisible assumptions (transparent priors) are the contexts not yet visited.

Primary practices. The structural difficulty is that attention cannot be aimed at what is invisible — by definition. All detection is therefore indirect, and the practice portfolio is a portfolio of indirections. Disruption: situations that violate a hidden assumption reveal it by the violation — novel environments, travel, unfamiliar social worlds, role reversals; retreats that break routine rather than deepen it; dark retreat and psychedelics (under the amplifier conditions in the opening note), which expose assumptions by removing the regularities that kept them fed. The read-out is surprise: anything that startles the system was holding something up. Other people as mirrors: others see your filter from outside — feedback-rich relationships, explicitly agreed to; a trusted teacher or peer; coaching and teaching others, whose blind spots reliably mirror your own; and the humbling genres — being edited, being beaten at something, being wrong in public. The certainty-audit: sweep for the felt-tones of "obviously," "of course," and "should." Certainty with flavor is a hidden assumption with investment behind it. The companion question: "What am I sure of right now — and what does the sureness feel like?" The what-gets-skimmed audit: boredom, faint irritation, mild contempt, subtle hurry — low-grade feelings that never quite become objects are the exhaust of a filtering assumption. Whatever you reliably skim past is being kept out of view by something.

Why it works — and how it goes wrong, which at this stage is subtle: disruption-seeking can quietly reconstitute as seeking (Structure 4 at a higher level); the auditor can harden into a new vigilance (Structure 3 re-imported — the assumption-hunter as the inner manager's final job); and the deepest failure: this framework itself becoming an invisible assumption — the lens through which practice is seen, filtering exactly as invisibly as anything it describes. The reminder that every map must eventually be set down is not decoration here; at this stage it is the practice. Signs it's working: genuine surprise, at intervals — the authentic "I did not know that was there," most often delivered by another person or a broken routine, and received with more curiosity than defense. There is no completion signature; the honest expectation is that this goes on indefinitely, asymptotically. When to change course: there is no next structure forward — but backward routing stays live: a discovered assumption that turns out to be protective routes to Structure 10's negotiation, not to more disruption.
